=====================================================
 Fortiq Community Edition (Windows x64)
 Independent, Zero-Secret Recovery Assurance Platform
=====================================================

QUICK START:
1. Open the "desktop" folder and run "Fortiq.Desktop.exe"
   (or launch Fortiq from the Start Menu after installation).
2. Follow the setup wizard to protect your files.

RECOVERY ON ANY PC:
You do not need an active Fortiq installation or service to restore:
- Open "desktop\Fortiq.Desktop.exe", choose "Run as Portable", and select "Recovery", or
- Launch emergency recovery using your 24-word recovery phrase and kit.json.

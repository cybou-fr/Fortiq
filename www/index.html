PROPRIETARY TOOLING

Our Secret Weapon: In-House Zero-Trust Tooling

Most IT providers rely on commercial SaaS tools (TeamViewer, AnyDesk, SolarWinds) that expose client servers to centralized cloud compromises and third-party vendor supply-chain attacks. FORTIQ engineered its own in-house Rust P2P software to give our clients mathematical security guarantees.

01

True Peer-to-Peer QUIC Streams

Direct authenticated transport with libp2p. Low latency UDP/QUIC streams replace slow TCP tunnels, delivering instantaneous interactive terminal execution with zero third-party cloud intermediaries.

02

DCUtR & Circuit Relay v2 Hole Punching

Works seamlessly behind CGNAT, strict enterprise NAT, and cellular modems without opening incoming firewall ports or creating attack surfaces on the public internet.

03

Cryptographic Ed25519 Identities

No central usernames, passwords, or master tokens that could leak in a SaaS data breach. Access authorization is strictly validated against immutable cryptographic keypairs.

04

Ticket-Gated Access Control

Our engineers can never connect uninvited. Every session requires an explicit cryptographically signed ticket opened directly on the customer's machine with automatic expiry.
